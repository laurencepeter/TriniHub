# Role feature draft

## Administrator
- Assign or update user access roles (administrator or corporation) with organization, display name, and email metadata.
- Review the current roster of access-controlled users and when access was last updated.
- View live issue analytics by status (pending, under review, in progress, completed).
- Track monthly completion summaries for resolved CivSnap reports.
- Access the full CivSnap command center alongside public reporting views.

## Corporation
- Monitor live issue volume with status summary metrics.
- Update report status as crews progress through review, work, and completion stages, including optional status comments.
- Review monthly completion summaries for resolved CivSnap reports.
- Access the CivSnap command center for operational reporting details.
- Review dog registration submissions and update pending registrations to approved or deleted.

## Public
- Browse community issue feed and search by keywords or description.
- Filter reports by status.
- Vote for open community issues.
- Submit new CivSnap reports with photos and location data.
